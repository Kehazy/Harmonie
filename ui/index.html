About

What Harmonie changes

A Spicetify theme, installed by a small app written in Rust.

Version-
EngineRust · Tauri 2
Size3 MB
Installed viaSpicetify

Harmonie only changes how Spotify looks. It does not touch ads or Premium features.

Blurred art

The colors of the current cover fill the background and change with every track.

Lyrics

Lyrics sit on the blurred cover, with the current line in bold white.

Frosted glass

Translucent panels, pill search bar and soft shadows on covers.

Floating player

A rounded glass card, or docked to the edge if you prefer.

Accent color

Rose, violet, blue, green or orange, in dark and light.
