About

What Harmonie changes

A Spicetify theme, installed by a small app written in Rust.

Version-
EngineRust · Tauri 2
Size3.0 MB
Installed viaSpicetify

Harmonie only changes how Spotify looks. It does not touch ads or Premium features.

Blurred art

The colors of the current cover fill the background and change with every track.

Frosted glass

Translucent panels and rounded corners across the interface.

Floating player

The player becomes a rounded card lifted off the edge.

Accent color

Rose, violet, blue, green or orange on playback and progress.

Typography

A fine system font, with tighter and more readable titles.
